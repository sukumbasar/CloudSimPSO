PSO SCHEDULING

In the rapidly evolving landscape of cloud computing, the efficiency of task scheduling remains a fundamental challenge that directly dictates system performance. The core of this problem lies in the complex mapping of workloads to virtual resources—a process so computationally intensive that traditional search methods simply cannot keep pace with its vast search space. While conventional static algorithms and simple heuristics often fall short due to their 'machine blindness' or their tendency to settle for local optima, our research pivots toward a more sophisticated, global approach. We have adopted Particle Swarm Optimization (PSO)—a meta-heuristic inspired by the collective intelligence of natural swarms—to navigate this complexity. Our decision to utilize PSO is rooted in its inherent flexibility and its capacity for continuous refinement. Rather than relying on a single-pass decision, our framework leverages an iterative, population-based search that evaluates the global landscape. To push these boundaries further, we developed a Hybrid Aging PSO model, integrating advanced enhancement mechanisms designed to overcome the limitations of standard meta-heuristics.

Scheduling Algorithms
6 total
FCFSStatic · Baseline
First-Come-First-Served — sequential assignment with no resource awareness. Lower-bound performance baseline across all 54 scenarios.
Round RobinStatic
Circular VM assignment. Produces results statistically equivalent to FCFS when all tasks arrive simultaneously at t=0.1 — empirically confirmed.
SJFStatic · Machine-Blind
Shortest Job First — sorts ascending by task length. Ignores VM speed. Underperforms FCFS in multiple scenarios: Machine-Blindness phenomenon (100/10: SJF=239s vs FCFS=217s).
Min-MinHeuristic
Assigns smallest tasks first to the VM with minimum completion time. Resource-aware. Wins extreme constrained scenarios (1000/10: 659s — beats Max-Min).
Max-MinHeuristic
Assigns largest tasks first to the VM with minimum completion time. Prevents bottlenecks. Wins most scenarios — primary PSO benchmark.
Hybrid Aging PSOMeta-heuristic ★
Population-based global search with 8 enhancement mechanisms. Primary research contribution. Finds solutions 28–31% better than CloudSim reflects across all 9 scenarios.
PSO Enhancement Mechanisms
8 mechanisms · iteratively added
Hybrid Initialization
Initialization
5 particles seeded with Max-Min + 5 with Min-Min. Provides high-quality starting points from both heuristic extremes, maximizing initial search space coverage.
Dynamic Inertia Weight
Core PSO · W: 0.9 → 0.4
Linearly decreasing W promotes global exploration early and local exploitation later. Replaces fixed W=0.7 from basic PSO. Based on Xue (2019).
Velocity Clamping
Stabilization · vMax = vmCount × 0.5
Bounds particle velocity to prevent overshooting. Scales with problem size — 10 VMs → ±5, 50 VMs → ±25. Confirmed by experiment to have no impact on CloudSim makespan.
Early Stopping
Convergence Detection · 20 iter plateau
Terminates when global best shows no improvement for 20 consecutive iterations. Confirmed PSO converges to plateau before 200-iteration limit — motivates Aging mechanism.
Aging Mechanism
Anti-Convergence · MAX_AGE = 15 · branch: main
Each particle tracks stagnation count. When age ≥ 15 without improvement, particle is rejuvenated with new random position. Directly addresses premature convergence.
Opposition-Based Learning
Init Enhancement · branch: pso-obl
For each particle computes opposite: pos_opp = (vmCount-1) - pos. Evaluates both and keeps better one. Doubles effective search coverage at initialization.
Cauchy Mutation
Escape Mechanism · 10% prob · branch: pso-cauchy
10% probability per particle: applies Cauchy-distributed random jump to one task assignment. Heavy-tailed distribution enables large escapes from local optima — reduces aging need.
Adaptive Inertia Weight
Dynamic Tuning · branch: pso-adaptivew
Adjusts W based on swarm diversity: diversity < 0.1 → W×1.1 (explore); diversity > 0.5 → W×0.9 (focus). Complements Dynamic W with real-time adaptation.
Lbest Topology
Social Structure · neighborhood=3 · branch: pso-full
Replaces gbest with lbest: each particle follows only 3 nearest neighbors. Prevents premature swarm collapse. Doubles rejuvenations in 1000/10 scenario (17→40).

Key Findings
~30%
Aging PSO internal fitness improvement across ALL 9 scenarios. PSO finds 28–31% better solutions than CloudSim's TimeShared scheduler reflects — confirmed via PSO_Internal_Makespan metric.
76%
Maximum makespan reduction by PSO over FCFS in the 500/10 scenario (1035s → 345s). Demonstrates critical value of meta-heuristic scheduling over static approaches.
❌ SJF
SJF proved Machine-Blind — worse than FCFS in 100/10 scenario (239s vs 217s). Sorting by task length without VM speed consideration causes systematic misallocation.
≈ RR
Round Robin and FCFS produce near-identical results when tasks arrive simultaneously at t=0.1. Circular ordering provides zero advantage under batch task submission.
65.55s
Best CloudSim makespan: 50 particles × 500 iterations in 100/10. 14% better than peer implementation best of 75.84s using different hybrid strategy.
96
Maximum rejuvenations in 1000/50 — aging mechanism actively combats premature convergence at scale. Lbest topology doubles rejuvenations in 1000/10 (17→40).
